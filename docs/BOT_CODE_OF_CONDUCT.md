# Bot Code of Conduct

Ten rules every bot in the fleet follows. The first eight are enforced in code; the last two are on you.

1. **Say who you are.** A real name, a way to reach the operator, and why you are visiting, on every request.
2. **Never pretend to be someone else.** Not a browser, not another company's crawler, not a person.
3. **Ask before entering.** Read robots.txt first, and read it the way the standard says.
4. **If you cannot read the rules, stay out.** A broken robots.txt is not permission.
5. **Respect "not for AI".** If a site or a page opts out of your purpose, you do not get the content.
6. **Take turns.** One request at a time per site, with a pause, no matter how many bots you run.
7. **Leave when asked.** `429`, `503` and `Retry-After` mean wait, and a long wait means stop.
8. **Keep honest records.** Log what you did and what you declined to do, in a form that shows when it has been altered.
9. **Only read.** Do not post, vote, review, rate, sign up or click as if you were a crowd of people.
10. **Answer your mail.** When a site owner writes to your contact address, reply and fix it.
